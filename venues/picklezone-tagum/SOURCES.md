## Conflicts to raise with the venue

- **Court count.** The desk lead says 5 courts (from the PickleHub grid). The venue's Nov 1 advisory adds premium courts 6–9. The site shows 5 now + 4 premium from 1 Dec. The desk lead should be updated to 9 once confirmed.
- **Rates.** The desk lead lists "₱250 listed; ₱275–₱300/hr on the schedule". The advisory explains this as time bands. The site uses the advisory.
- **Premium start date.** "Pre-bookings for December" is read as bookable from 1 Dec 2026. Confirm.
- **Premium courts' location** is not on the published layout, so the site draws them off-plan and says so.
- **Facebook URL.** The desk lead has a profile.php link; PickleHub links facebook.com/picklezonetagum. The site uses the PickleHub link.
